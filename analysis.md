## What it is and who it is for

Gable Guardians is a 2D pixel-art MMORPG on Solana. You play as a gargoyle - there are no classes; your stat points (STR, STA, DEX, INT) and your weapon define the role. The world has 60+ maps: combat zones 2-8, a Dragon Dungeon, four elemental cities, a PvP arena and Guild Wars. It runs on web (no download), Solana Seeker, PlaySolana PSG1 and Android.

The audience is gamers who want real ownership without friction: your character, weapons, armor and skills are compressed NFTs on Solana, and the gargoyles awakened at The Altar become 1:1 Metaplex Core NFTs you can keep, trade or sell. Your wallet holds them, not the game.

## Live today vs planned

Live now: alpha v0.3.72, level cap 100 (starting at 1), the full hunting and crafting loop, daily missions from level 5, skill crafting from level 10, PvP ranking from level 11, and The Altar transformation at level 35. Right now the Samhain Halloween event runs October 1-31 (UTC): pacts paid in GABLE, daily rituals, and a token pool of 20,000 SKR + 200,000 PLAY distributed across the month.

Planned: a maximum level of 1000, adding roughly 100 levels per year. No published dates for later systems.

## Token utility and tokenomics

The economy is multi-token: GABLE, PLAY, SKR, SOL and GOLD (gold-backed, withdrawable as XAUt0). GABLE is the workhorse of the economy, per the official docs: NFT mints cost the equivalent of 0.01-0.015 SOL; tool crafting and repairs; utility scrolls priced from a base SOL value at the live GABLE/SOL rate; guild contributions (burn GABLE worth 0.001 SOL for +10 guild XP, plus 0.01 SOL maintenance every 27 days); stylist skins at $0.65; Relic Chests; and the Halloween pacts. The item marketplace charges a 5% fee and supports GABLE buy orders; NFT trading routes through Tensor.

## One meaningful risk or unanswered question

Liquidity is thin. The two GABLE pools hold roughly $18K combined and 24-hour volume was about $51 at the time of writing (DexScreener, Oct 16, 2026). Exits of any meaningful size will move the price, and that raises a question the docs do not answer: where does durable demand for GABLE come from as the player base grows - active players spending in-game, or mostly speculation? A second open question is retention past level 100, since the level treadmill restarts with about 100 levels per year.
